🥗 Dietary & Nutritional Strategy
  • Adopt a Mediterranean dietary pattern emphasizing leafy greens, berries, legumes, and extra virgin olive oil.
  • Limit saturated fats to under 6% of daily caloric intake and eliminate trans fats to protect the arterial endothelial lining.
  • Incorporate soluble viscous fiber (oats, psyllium, beans) daily to actively bind bile acids and lower LDL cholesterol.
🏃 Physical Activity & Conditioning
  • Aim for at least 150 minutes of moderate-intensity aerobic physical activity weekly (brisk walking, swimming, cycling).
  • Target safe aerobic training zone: 50%–70% of age-predicted maximum heart rate.
  • Incorporate 2 days per week of moderate resistance training to enhance insulin sensitivity and arterial compliance.
🩺 Clinical & Diagnostic Next Steps
  • Schedule routine annual cardiovascular screening with your primary care physician.
  • Monitor resting blood pressure and heart rate on a quarterly basis.
  • Consider an advanced lipid particle panel (ApoB and Lp(a)) to assess residual atherogenic risk.
